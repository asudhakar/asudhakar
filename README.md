### About me

Senior software engineer and team lead with **10+ years** building and architecting
backend systems, microservices, and distributed platforms. I'm a polyglot — fluent in
**Go**, **TypeScript/JavaScript**, **Python**, and **Java** — and like picking the right
tool for the problem.

At **Thoughtworks**, I lead squads of 5–8 engineers delivering high-scale fintech platforms
(loan origination, loan management, banking security) on **Kubernetes**, architecting services
that handle **1M+ requests/day at sub-100ms latency**. I care deeply about clean architecture,
design patterns, and rigorous peer review — and I apply generative AI across the SDLC, from an
LLM-powered code-review system to shipping production systems with self-hosted AI coding agents.

- 🔭 Currently building **[Vasool.App](https://vasool.app)** — a multi-tenant loan management SaaS for microfinance
- 🤖 Working at the intersection of distributed systems and **generative AI** in the SDLC
- 🌱 Always learning across languages and stacks
- ✍️ I write at [sudhakarannadurai.wordpress.com](https://sudhakarannadurai.wordpress.com/)
- 📫 Reach me at **asudhakar@live.in**

### 🚀 Featured project

**[Vasool.App](https://vasool.app)** — a multi-tenant loan management SaaS for Indian microfinance companies.

- 🏗️ Clean layered REST API in **Go** (Gin, GORM; Handler → Service → Repository) supporting 8+ loan products with JWT auth and per-tenant feature flags
- 🐳 Isolated **Docker + PostgreSQL** environments per tenant behind a Caddy reverse proxy, with automated provisioning and **GitHub Actions** CI/CD
- 📱 **React Native** (Expo) field-collection app in 6 Indian languages with offline-tolerant flows, a **Next.js** customer portal, and a standalone Go UPI payment microservice
